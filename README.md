## ✦ Executive Profile & Architectural Ethos

<img align="right" alt="Developer Workstation" src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/abhisheknaiidu/abhisheknaiidu/master/code.gif" width="370" />

<p align="left">
I am <strong>Mahfuz Hasan</strong>, a full-stack software architect specializing in the design and delivery of modern distributed web platforms, high-throughput microservices, and AI-augmented developer workflows.
</p>

<p align="left">
My engineering philosophy is rooted in <strong>Claude-inspired editorial minimalism</strong>: stripping away extraneous complexity to produce systems characterized by <strong>strict type rigor</strong>, <strong>sub-second P95 latencies</strong>, and <strong>bulletproof concurrency safety</strong>. Whether structuring multi-tenant RBAC permissions or orchestrating autonomous agent loops, I build software that scales reliably under production load.
</p>

### ⚜️ Strategic Focus & Core Initiatives:
- 🏢 **Production Distributed Systems:** Architecting peer-to-peer marketplaces and booking platforms with **Next.js 16 (App Router)**, **React 19**, and **Express/MongoDB** in [`StudioSpace`](https://github.com/mahfuzhasan2700/studiospace-client).
- 🛒 **Market Data & Intelligence:** Engineering real-time price indexes and grocery commodity trackers with **Next.js 16**, **Tailwind CSS v4**, and **MongoDB Atlas** in [`Bazar-Dor`](https://github.com/mahfuzhasan2700/Bazar-Dor).
- ⚡ **Reactive State Analytics:** Implementing habit metrics and live telemetry calculators with reactive states in [`Fit-Log`](https://github.com/mahfuzhasan2700/Assignment-6).
- 🤖 **Agentic AI & Tooling:** Developing autonomous multi-step reasoning agents and tool-calling execution pipelines in [`Perseus_AI`](https://github.com/mahfuzhasan2700/Perseus_AI).
- 🎯 **Trigonometric Physics Simulators:** Calculating projectile ballistics and vector trajectory arcs at 60FPS on canvas in [`artillery-laying-simulator`](https://github.com/mahfuzhasan2700/artillery-laying-simulator).
- 📬 **Direct Inquiries:** Available via [mahfuzhasan2700@gmail.com](mailto:mahfuzhasan2700@gmail.com) and on [LinkedIn](https://linkedin.com/in/mahfuzhasan2700).
